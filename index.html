2Sample a natural word or phrase NEW

Write a word or short sentence in one natural motion. When the same text appears in your notes, the program uses your real connected strokes instead of assembling individual letters. For new text it uses your letter samples. This runs offline; it does not train an AI model.

Write the phrase as you normally would, left to right, between the guide lines. Avoid squeezing long sentences into the box; shorter samples work best.

3Write your content

Type or paste your notes. The program builds lines and automatically adds more pages when needed, using the selected paper size.

Uppercase conversion is enabled. Switch it off to use lowercase samples in your notes.

Accented letters work directly in notes and equations. Try NIÑO, niño, mañana, lápiz, café, naïve. Each accent uses your saved base letter and accent-mark sample, with no separate Ñ or ñ sample needed.

Saved phrase matches will appear here after you start sampling.

Math notation + your handwriting

Build a formula below, then insert it at the cursor in your notes. Use $...$ for an inline equation or $$...$$ for a centered equation on its own line. You can also type LaTeX directly in your notes.

45% · natural

Adds tiny, smooth pen wobbles and imperfect corners to equation lines. 0% gives straight ruler lines. The same pattern is used in PDF output.

Square-root signs now stretch over the full expression inside their braces. For example, use \sqrt{x+\int_0^L f(t)dt} to place the integral under the root.
